I am a Software Engineering master's student at **Nanjing University**, working with the **Language Intelligence Processing Laboratory (LipLab)**. I build reliable agent systems that coordinate across long-horizon tasks, learn from prior experience, and take grounded actions in real interfaces.

<div class="notice--primary">
  <strong>Research focus:</strong> long-horizon agent systems, computer-use agents, agentic RAG, and multimodal reasoning and evaluation.
</div>

## Current Position

I am currently an Algorithm Intern in the **Multimodal LLM Department at SenseTime**, where I develop data and evaluation pipelines for presentation agents and contribute to research on revision-aware multi-agent presentation authoring.
